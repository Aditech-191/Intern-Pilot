InternPilot — Never Miss an Opportunity That Fits You

InternPilot is an AI-powered career and opportunity navigator built for Ugandan university students—starting with software engineering undergraduates—who constantly miss out on internships and scholarships because listings are buried across noisy WhatsApp groups, LinkedIn posts, and campus notice boards.

How It Works

Smart Opportunity Ingestion: Paste any messy, unstructured WhatsApp forward or LinkedIn post, and Google Gemini instantly extracts clean, structured details—title, organization, deadline, eligibility, and requirements.

Profile-Matched Feed: Students get a mobile-first, low-bandwidth feed with real-time deadline countdowns filtered specifically to their course, year of study, and technical skills, with one-tap Save and Applied tracking.

Grounded AI Career Advisor: A personalized Gemini chatbot evaluates opportunities strictly against the student's profile and live database listings—delivering tailored application advice with zero hallucinated links or fake deadlines.

Built For Impact

Designed specifically for students in Uganda, InternPilot turns fragmented campus information into an actionable, deadline-driven launchpad for early tech careers.
